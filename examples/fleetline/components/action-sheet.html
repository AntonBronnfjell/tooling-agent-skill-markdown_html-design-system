Action sheet

Usage

Use an action sheet on a phone or tablet when a mechanic picks one action for one item: a work order's Mark done, Reassign, Print. It opens from the item's More actions button or from a long press.

  • Action sheet — one-shot actions on one item, 2 to 6 of them, plus Cancel.
  • Don't use it for navigation, toggles or settings (use a menu), for a form (use a modal), or to confirm a destructive action (use an alert dialog).

Content: verb + object ("Reassign mechanic", "Delete work order"). The title is the item's name when the trigger doesn't say it. Cancel is always the last row.

Anatomy

  1. Backdrop — ::backdrop with --color-bg-overlay
  2. Container — <dialog class="action-sheet"> on the overlay surface, bottom edge on phones
  3. Header (optional) — .action-sheet__title and .action-sheet__message
  4. Actions — .action-sheet__item buttons, --danger for destructive ones (last)
  5. Cancel — after .action-sheet__separator, closes without acting

Examples

Open sheets are previewed in the page with the docs-only .is-preview class; every demo also has a real trigger that opens it in the top layer with commandfor/command="show-modal", without JavaScript.

Do and don't

Accessibility

Keyboard interaction
KeyBehavior
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between the actions and Cancel; the page behind is inert
Enter / SpaceRuns the focused action and closes the sheet
EscCloses without acting (same as Cancel)
  • Role: native <dialog> opened with showModal() (via the invoker): top layer, inert background, Esc. Named by the title or by aria-label.
  • Focus: moves to the first action, or to Cancel (autofocus) when a destructive action is present. On close the browser returns focus to the trigger.
  • Targets: every row is at least --size-touch-target tall and full width.
  • Destructive actions are named in text ("Delete work order"), not by color alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
  • Reduced motion: the slide is removed by base.css. Forced colors: the sheet keeps a CanvasText border.
